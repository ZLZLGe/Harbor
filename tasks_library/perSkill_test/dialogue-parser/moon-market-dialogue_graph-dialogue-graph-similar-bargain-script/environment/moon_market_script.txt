[MoonMarketStart]
Narrator: Silver lanterns sway over the moon market as Mira beckons you toward a crescent-shaped stall. -> LanternGreeting

[LanternGreeting]
Mira: Looking for a tideglass lantern? This one still remembers the sea. -> PriceMenu

[PriceMenu]
1. Ask the asking price. -> FirstPrice
2. [Appraise] Inspect the lantern frame and crystal seams. -> AppraiseLantern
3. [Bluff] Mention a cheaper offer from the next stall. -> RivalBluff
4. Walk away and browse the market. -> WalkAway

[FirstPrice]
Mira: Sixty silver crescents, with a fresh wick and moon-oil vial. -> BargainMenu

[AppraiseLantern]
Narrator: The brass is honest work, but one rune ring has a hairline crack near the hinge. -> AppraiseReply

[AppraiseReply]
Mira: Sharp eyes. That crack does not dim the light, but it does give us room to talk. -> BargainMenu

[BargainMenu]
1. Offer forty silver crescents. -> LowOffer
2. Offer fifty-five silver crescents. -> FairOffer
3. [Charm] Praise Mira's craftsmanship before naming a price. -> CharmPitch
4. Ask why this lantern is worth more than the others. -> StoryPitch

[LowOffer]
Mira: Forty? I'd lose money before moonrise. -> LowOfferChoice

[LowOfferChoice]
1. Hold firm at forty. -> BrokerLaughs
2. [Moon Lore] Point out the cracked rune ring and old tideglass. -> LoreDiscount
3. Raise the offer to fifty. -> MidOffer
4. Step back and restart the conversation. -> PriceMenu

[BrokerLaughs]
Broker: At forty, you may as well ask Mira to gift-wrap the moon. -> EmbarrassedReset

[EmbarrassedReset]
Mira: Ignore him. If you want the lantern, make me a serious offer. -> BargainMenu

[LoreDiscount]
Mira: Hm. Someone taught you the old marks. Fifty-two, and I'll stop pretending that crack is decorative. -> LoreChoice

[LoreChoice]
1. Accept fifty-two. -> DealAccepted
2. Ask for a spare wick as well. -> AddOnTalk
3. Say you need another lap around the market. -> WalkAway

[MidOffer]
Mira: Fifty gets my attention, but not the lantern. -> MidOfferChoice

[MidOfferChoice]
1. Add a promise to recommend her stall. -> ReputationDeal
2. [Signal] Nod to the apprentice to speak up for the lantern. -> ApprenticePitch
3. Return to the higher offer. -> FairOffer

[FairOffer]
Mira: Fifty-five is respectable. We are finally speaking the same language. -> FairOfferChoice

[FairOfferChoice]
1. Take the deal. -> DealAccepted
2. Ask for a carrying hook included. -> AddOnTalk
3. [Signal] Invite the apprentice to sweeten the sale. -> ApprenticePitch

[CharmPitch]
Mira: Flattery buys you a smile, not a discount... unless you keep talking. -> CharmChoice

[CharmChoice]
1. Offer fifty-two with a grin. -> DealAccepted
2. Push your luck and ask for forty-eight. -> CharmBackfire
3. Ask for a travel hook instead of a lower price. -> AddOnTalk

[CharmBackfire]
Mira: Forty-eight and a grin? Bold. Try again when you bring better poetry. -> BargainMenu

[StoryPitch]
Mira: This lantern guided my sister through the eclipse floods. It shines steady when maps fail. -> StoryChoice

[StoryChoice]
1. Buy it for the quoted price. -> DealAccepted
2. Ask the broker whether the story is true. -> BrokerTruth
3. Circle the square and come back later. -> PriceMenu

[BrokerTruth]
Broker: The story is true enough. The lantern works, but Mira is charging for the legend too. -> TruthChoice

[TruthChoice]
1. Use that admission to counter at fifty-three. -> TruthCounter
2. Thank them both and leave. -> WalkAway
3. Decide the story is worth the full price. -> DealAccepted

[TruthCounter]
Mira: Fifty-three, no hook, no extra oil. Final moonrise offer. -> FinalChoice

[FinalChoice]
1. Accept the final offer. -> DealAccepted
2. Refuse and leave. -> WalkAway

[AddOnTalk]
Apprentice: We can include a carrying hook if the sale closes before the bell. -> AddOnChoice

[AddOnChoice]
1. Agree to the current price with the hook. -> DealAccepted
2. Try to shave off two more silver. -> GuardWarning
3. Decide against the purchase. -> WalkAway

[ReputationDeal]
Mira: Bring me two customers tomorrow, and fifty will do tonight. -> ReputationChoice

[ReputationChoice]
1. Accept the promise deal. -> DealAccepted
2. Decide you cannot promise that. -> BargainMenu

[ApprenticePitch]
Apprentice: Mistress, the cracked ring means we should move it before the damp gets worse. -> ApprenticeChoice

[ApprenticeChoice]
1. Offer fifty-one silver now. -> FinalChoice
2. Thank the apprentice and return to the main negotiation. -> BargainMenu

[RivalBluff]
Mira: Then the next stall is selling fog in a bottle. If your quote is real, prove it. -> BluffChoice

[BluffChoice]
1. [Bluff] Insist the other merchant offered forty-five. -> BluffPressed
2. Admit you exaggerated. -> BargainMenu
3. Leave to compare stalls. -> WalkAway

[BluffPressed]
Mira: Forty-five and no warranty, perhaps. I can do fifty-four if you stop performing for the crowd. -> FinalChoice

[GuardWarning]
Guard: Keep the haggling civil. The bell is about to ring, and the lane stays open only for honest trade. -> GuardChoice

[GuardChoice]
1. Apologize and accept the standing offer. -> DealAccepted
2. Take the hint and leave. -> WalkAway

[DealAccepted]
Mira: Done. The lantern, hook if promised, and fresh oil are yours. -> End

[WalkAway]
Narrator: You melt back into the moon market crowd, still weighing silver against starlight. -> End
