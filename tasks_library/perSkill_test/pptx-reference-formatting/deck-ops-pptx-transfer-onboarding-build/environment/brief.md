## Workflow
Title: From Intake to Resolution
Intro: Show the operating flow as four connected stages.

- Queue triage | Clarify channel, customer tier, and severity before work starts. | Owner: Duty specialist
- Diagnose | Reproduce the issue and gather the missing facts in one thread. | Owner: Primary investigator
- Escalate | Route product or billing blockers with the right context and urgency. | Owner: Escalation lead
- Close the loop | Confirm next steps, document the outcome, and update the customer. | Owner: Case owner
