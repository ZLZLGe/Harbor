Glacier deployment memo
=======================

Site: North margin icefall array, midsummer melt season.

Operational context:
- This hour is the first continuous test after moving the array closer to the icefall. There is no reviewed local icequake catalog yet.
- The usable channels are mixed: higher-gain BH/HH seismic channels plus HN strong-motion channels. Analysts want one first-pass candidate list that can generalize across that mixed hardware.
- The network is sparse and local noise changes quickly as meltwater channels migrate. Simple amplitude-only triggers are producing many extra detections during slush bursts and calving noise.
- We do not have stable local templates from this glacier front, so methods that depend on pre-existing waveform families are not expected to transfer well.

What the analyst wants:
- A practical first-pass candidate schedule for follow-up review, not a perfect final catalog.
- Recovery of smaller mixed-sensor icequakes matters more than ultra-low latency.
- Calibration and telemetry service windows must be excluded from the review queue.
