Do Things that Don't Scale

At the beginning, founders cannot rely on scale. They need to win trust person by person and learn from each interaction. The work feels manual, but that is exactly why it reveals details that automated funnels hide.

In the early days, speed of learning matters more than elegance. If a startup listens closely, each customer conversation becomes product research. That loop is worth more than a polished dashboard with no adoption signal.

When teams avoid direct contact, they miss weak signals. A short call, a custom setup, or a messy migration often uncovers blockers before churn appears in a report [1].

Founders should still design for future leverage, but they should not wait for perfect systems. Build lightweight tools, watch real usage, and keep fixing friction. Reference link: https://paulgraham.com/ds.html
