Founder Mode

Some organizations lose energy when every decision gets flattened into generic management process. Founder mode means staying close to the product details long enough to preserve the original quality bar.

Delegation is necessary, but delegation without context transfer creates drift. Teams execute local goals while the larger direction fades, and the product starts to feel fragmented.

A founder can restore alignment by asking concrete questions, reviewing edge cases, and clarifying priorities in plain language. This is not micromanagement when the goal is coherence [2].

The useful pattern is alternating between depth and leverage: dive into critical paths, then codify what was learned so others can move faster. See also http://example.org/reference for unrelated notes.
