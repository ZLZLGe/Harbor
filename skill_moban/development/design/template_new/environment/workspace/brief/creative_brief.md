# AtlasFlow Review Launch Storyboard

## Context

AtlasFlow Review is a B2B creative-operations product for marketing, brand, and content teams that need to move review-heavy launch work from scattered files and ad hoc approval threads into a single workflow.

The leadership team wants a browser-native launch storyboard that can be opened directly in a desktop browser and reviewed live in weekly go-to-market meetings. This is not a PDF export exercise and not a long-scroll landing page. The final deck must feel like a concise internal launch narrative that balances business momentum with operational realism.

## Primary audience

- VP Marketing
- Director of Brand Operations
- Head of Creative Production
- Launch PM and regional campaign leads

## Questions the deck must answer

1. Why is AtlasFlow Review worth launching now?
2. Which operating signals show traction or readiness?
3. How does the product compare against familiar alternatives in review-heavy workflows?
4. What customer evidence supports the story, and what are its limits?
5. What does the intended review journey look like from intake to sign-off?
6. What risks or scope boundaries still need explicit acknowledgement?

## Narrative priorities

- Lead with one clear conclusion rather than a neutral summary.
- Make the KPI page visually legible in a live review setting.
- Keep comparison language grounded in the supplied matrix. Avoid vague superiority claims.
- Show the review journey as a real workflow with handoffs, not as abstract circles.
- End with risks and next steps that feel credible to operations leaders.

## Forbidden claims

- Do not claim that AtlasFlow Review replaces every project management system.
- Do not claim “fully autonomous approvals”, “zero revisions”, or “one-click compliance”.
- Do not claim support for external vendor portals or multilingual review if not grounded in source inputs.
- Do not attribute quantified customer outcomes unless the quote payload or KPI data explicitly supports them.

## Style direction

- Confident, editorial, and boardroom-friendly.
- Avoid generic startup hype language.
- Keep visual density controlled: each slide should be readable without scrolling.
- The look and tone should feel consistent with the mirrored marketing materials under `/app/workspace/mirror/site/`.
