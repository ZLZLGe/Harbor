# Draft Claims To Verify

The draft citation keys are provisional and may point to duplicated, noisy, or irrelevant records in `seed_library.bib`.

## C01

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines a parametric sequence-to-sequence model with a non-parametric memory of retrieved documents, and was evaluated on knowledge-intensive NLP tasks. Draft citation: `lewis2020rag`.

## C02

REALM introduced retrieval-augmented language model pre-training with latent retrieved documents and reported improvements on open-domain question answering benchmarks. Draft citation: `guu2020realm`.

## C03

Dense Passage Retrieval uses a dual-encoder dense retriever as an alternative to sparse lexical retrieval for open-domain question answering. Draft citation: `karpukhin2020dpr`.

## C04

Fusion-in-Decoder is the paper that established clinical safety standards for medical question answering systems. Draft citation: `izacard2021fid`.

## C05

HyDE proves that hypothetical document generation eliminates the need for relevance labels in every retrieval domain. Draft citation: `gao2023hyde`.

## C06

Adding retrieval fully solves hallucination in retrieval-augmented generation systems. Draft citation: `lewis2020rag`.

## C07

Med-PaLM is an in-scope retrieval-augmented generation architecture paper for open-domain question answering. Draft citation: `singhal2023medpalm`.

## C08

All accepted studies in this packet report human-participant experiments. Draft citation: `all_sources`.

## C09

Dense Passage Retrieval and HyDE make the same supervision assumption because both require relevance labels for training. Draft citation: `karpukhin2020dpr; gao2023hyde`.

## C10

REALM treats retrieved documents as latent variables during retrieval-augmented pre-training. Draft citation: `guu2020realm`.

## C11

The Transformer paper should be accepted as a core in-scope source for this packet because it is foundational for modern NLP. Draft citation: `vaswani2017attention`.

## C12

Fusion-in-Decoder removes retrieval coverage as a methodological limitation in open-domain question answering. Draft citation: `izacard2021fid`.
