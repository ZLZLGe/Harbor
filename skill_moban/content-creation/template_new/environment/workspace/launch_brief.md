# SignalLeaf Studio 2.0 launch brief

## Release summary

SignalLeaf is launching `Studio 2.0` on **April 28, 2026** for content operations leads and lean B2B marketing teams that repurpose long-form recordings into multiple distribution formats each week.

The release goal is to position Studio 2.0 as a **content repurposing workflow** product for teams that already have webinars, podcasts, or interviews, but lose time when they have to turn one recording into multiple review-ready deliverables.

## Confirmed product facts

- Studio 2.0 turns one webinar or podcast source into a blog draft, a LinkedIn post, a newsletter draft, and clip-title suggestions.
- The new workflow adds an approval queue with role-based comments before anything is marked ready for handoff.
- At launch, Studio 2.0 is available on the **Growth** and **Scale** plans only.
- Studio 2.0 is **English only at launch**.
- Beta customers reported an average **42% faster weekly content packaging** process after moving their approval step into Studio 2.0.
- The official launch CTA is the waitlist URL: `https://signalleaf.example.com/studio-waitlist`.

## Do not claim

- Do not say the product is available on the Free plan.
- Do not say the product supports 20 or 20+ languages.
- Do not say the product auto-publishes or schedules to every social platform.
- Do not say the product automatically edits long-form video.
- Do not turn roadmap ideas or internal evaluation notes into launch claims.

## Messaging angle

- Lead with workflow clarity and review speed, not with hype.
- The audience already knows how to create content; they need fewer approval bottlenecks and cleaner repurposing handoffs.
- Treat the approval queue and multi-output workflow as the core differentiators.
- Keep launch messaging scoped to what is confirmed now, not what the team may ship later.
