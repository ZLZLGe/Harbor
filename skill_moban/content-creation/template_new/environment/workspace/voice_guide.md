# Voice guide

## Tone

- Calm, informed, and practical.
- Write like a content operations lead talking to another operator.
- Prefer concrete workflow language over broad marketing language.

## Style rules

- State what changes, why it matters, and who it helps.
- Use real numbers and plan details only when they are confirmed in the brief.
- Keep sentences direct; avoid slogans and filler.
- Do not promise outcomes that the brief does not support.

## Hard bans

- `revolutionary`
- `game-changing`
- `10x`
- `magical`
- `instantly publish everywhere`
- `free forever`
