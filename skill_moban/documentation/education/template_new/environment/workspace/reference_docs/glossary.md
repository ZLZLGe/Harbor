# Glossary

- `session_started`: the learner opened the workshop and the platform recorded the start event.
- `lesson_completed`: the learner reached the end of the main lesson path.
- `practice_opened`: the learner opened the practice activity after the lesson.
- `practice_submitted`: the learner submitted at least one practice response.
- `quiz_pass_rate`: share of unique learners with at least one passing quiz attempt out of unique learners who attempted the quiz.
- `retry_rate`: share of unique learners with more than one quiz attempt out of unique learners who attempted the quiz.
