# Working notes

PostHog presents itself as a platform for product analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, surveys, data pipelines, and related product engineering workflows.
The site speaks to builders who want one place to inspect product usage and ship changes with evidence.

## Audience and product shape

The product is aimed at engineering and product teams that care about fast iteration.
It combines analytics and shipping tools so teams can connect behavior, release decisions, and follow-up changes.
This makes it a useful supporting source for content about observing product loops instead of guessing.

## Messaging cues

The product language is practical and tool-oriented.
It favors direct claims about what teams can inspect, measure, or ship.
It gives the writing permission to talk about instrumentation, workflows, and product evidence.

## Content-use implications

This packet supports references to product analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, surveys, and data pipelines.
It does not support claims about market leadership, rankings, or guaranteed outcomes.
