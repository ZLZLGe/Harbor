Title: The hidden danger of shipping fast
Document kind: voice_sample

The writing in this piece is willing to challenge a celebrated operating mantra.
It does that by pointing to second-order effects, not by moralizing.

Speed matters, but only when the team can absorb what happened next.
Unchecked velocity can hide quality debt, weak review loops, and silent user pain.
The source keeps returning to feedback mechanisms and recovery paths.

Use this sample for balanced critique, compact setup lines, and insistence on guardrails around speed.
