Title: How we choose technologies
Document kind: voice_sample

We do not start with fashion.
We start with the job and the cost of being wrong.

The writing style in this piece is compact and direct.
Claims are followed by criteria, not by slogans.
Tradeoffs stay visible.
If a choice adds operational drag later, the piece says so plainly.

The source voice is comfortable saying that a tool is good in one context and poor in another.
It does not hide behind abstract best practices.
It also avoids trying to sound neutral when the team has a clear opinion.

Use this sample for sentence rhythm, admission of tradeoffs, and preference for concrete decision criteria.
