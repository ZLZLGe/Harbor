# Source notes

## Beta feedback excerpts

- Team A used to move webinar notes between docs, Slack, and email. Their ops lead said the approval queue cut handoff delays because editors and demand gen could comment in one place.
- Team B used Studio 2.0 during beta for a weekly podcast workflow and reported a 42% faster weekly content packaging cycle after switching the approval step into the product.

## Product notes

- The launch focuses on repurposing already-recorded webinars, podcasts, and interviews.
- Clip-title suggestions are included, but the product does not claim to edit full long-form video.
- Launch coverage is intentionally narrow: Growth and Scale plans, English only, waitlist CTA.

## Internal roadmap notes

- Multilingual output is being evaluated for a later release, but it is not part of the launch.
- The team has discussed lighter entry pricing, but Free plan availability is not confirmed.
- Distribution connectors for direct social scheduling are on the long-term roadmap, not in Studio 2.0 launch scope.

## Editorial reminders

- The audience already knows what repurposing is. The bundle should focus on reducing review friction and packaging delays.
- Avoid "AI saves everything" framing. Keep the copy operational and grounded.
- Treat roadmap notes as internal context only. They should not appear as launch claims in final deliverables.
